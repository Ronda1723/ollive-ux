How Ollive evaluates

Summary of the evaluation process; adversarial techniques and prompts are withheld. Legal interpretation and methodology reviewed in consultation with Crowell & Moring.

The evaluation pipelinemethodology v1.8 · packs versioned per run
1
Obligation mapping

Applicable laws and regulations, Confido's internal agent policies, and customer-facing commitments are decomposed into testable obligations. Applicability derives from the agent's workflows, tools, data access, and the jurisdictions it serves.

2
Scenario generation

Each obligation is tested with scenarios spanning cooperative, confused, frustrated, and adversarial callers, including elderly speech, interruptions, and ambiguous requests. Scenarios that fail ambiguously are refined and re-run.

3
Simulation

Multi-turn, voice-native interactions run against the live agent build, including audio conditions, interruptions and tool calls. Each attempt records its persona, conditions, and seed for reproducibility.

4
Judging

A vendor-diverse panel of three model judges scores each interaction against the obligation's rubric, with a 2-of-3 consensus rule. Judges are calibrated against human expert labels; judge splits go to human adjudication.

5
Banding & certification

Dimension results are reported as bands, not decimals. Certification is decided against the published thresholds below.

6
Remediation & re-verification

Findings are reported to Confido with root cause and suggested fix. A finding is marked resolved only after the failing scenarios are re-run against the updated agent version.

7
Monitoring

For certified agents in production, the same checks run against live conversations. Unpredicted production failures become permanent regression scenarios; the simulation-to-production gap is tracked.

Needs monitoringWithin threshold, but variance or context-sensitivity warrants scheduled production sampling.
Needs hardeningA control exists but failed in edge conditions; a remediation plan is agreed and tracked.
Remediation requiredBlocks certification until fixed and re-verified. None open across Confido's evaluated agents.
Certification levels
CertifiedAll dimensions at Meets threshold or above; no conditions. Annual re-evaluation.
Certified with monitoringThreshold met with a named monitoring condition (e.g. monthly billing-accuracy sampling). Bi-monthly review.
Conditionally certifiedBaseline met; one or more dimensions carry monitoring or hardening requirements. Quarterly review.
Not certifiedThreshold not met for the assessed use case. Not shown on this portal unless Confido chooses to share it.
