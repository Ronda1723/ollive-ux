How Ollive uses this table. Your disclosure profile (states, industry, call direction, user ages) selects which rows apply; scenario packs map to each applicable row; every violation found is priced from this table's per-violation and accrual mechanics — that's the liability number on Risk & liability. Amounts are statutory maxima or adjusted figures as of mid-2026; HIPAA, FTC and CCPA figures inflation-adjust annually; EU high-risk dates reflect the Digital Omnibus deferral. This is a modelled reference, not legal advice — counsel reviews before anything is represented to a regulator or buyer.
